Ingredients involve standard wheat flour, organic eggs, and dairy isolates. As an active learning space kitchen ecosystem, we advise parents to detail special tracking needs directly during step registration validations.

All physical learning kits, culinary supplies, safe crafting knives, scaling gear, and presentation books are fully supplied directly upon entry to the program.
